# Records

A Record is the time you actually spent — as opposed to a Plan, which is the time you intended to spend. Dayopt keeps these two separate: Plans live in their own lane on the calendar, Records live in another. Nothing becomes a Record automatically; you decide what gets recorded.

## Where a Record comes from

A Record can be linked to a Plan, or it can stand on its own:

- **Linked to a Plan** — created from a Plan you already scheduled. Dayopt keeps the connection so it can show the difference between what you planned and what you did.
- **Unplanned** — created directly for something you did that was never on the calendar. It shows up in the Record lane with a quiet "unplanned" marker, and nothing else.

A single Plan can have more than one Record — for example, if you worked on something, stepped away, and came back to it later.

## Turning a Plan into a Record

Once a Plan's time has passed, it stays an **unrecorded Plan** until you do one of the following:

| Method                      | How                                                                                        |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **One tap "Record as-is"**  | Tap the button on the Plan card — it copies the Plan's time range into a new Record        |
| **Drag to the Record lane** | Drag the Plan card into the Record lane; resize it if you want the recorded time to differ |
| **"Confirm this day"**      | Confirm all of a day's unrecorded Plans into Records at once, in one action                |

If you didn't do something, mark the Plan as **skipped** instead — Review keeps "skipped" and "unrecorded" as separate states.

## Creating a Record directly

You don't have to start from a Plan. Create a block on the calendar for a time range that has already ended, and Dayopt saves it as a Record automatically — there's no "save as Plan or Record" choice to make. The rule is simple: if the end time is in the future, it's a Plan; if it's now or in the past, it's a Record. The label on the block updates by itself as you adjust the time.

## Comparing Plans and Records

When a Record is linked to a Plan, Dayopt shows the difference as a number next to it — for example, how much longer or shorter the actual time was. A perfect match (±0) shows nothing at all, since that's simply what you expected. Unplanned Records never show a difference; they just carry their quiet marker.

Past Plans keep their scheduled time frozen for this comparison to stay meaningful — but you can still correct the title, tag, or note on a past Plan at any time.

## Editing a Record

Click any Record to open the Inspector, where you can adjust:

- **Title, tag, and note** — same fields as a Plan
- **Start / end time** — since a Record reflects something that already happened, you're free to correct it
