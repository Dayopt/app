# Connect Google Calendar

Connect Google Calendar and its events show up faintly on your Dayopt calendar. They are not plans or records yet — just a reference. You can see work and personal events side by side, then pull in only the ones you care about.

## Connecting an account

1. Open **Settings**, then the **Integrations** tab
2. Under Google Calendar, click **Connect Google account**
3. Sign in with your Google account and grant access
4. Check the calendars you want to bring in, then click **Apply**

While Google review is in progress, the sign-in screen may show an "unverified app" warning. Click **Advanced**, then choose to continue to Dayopt to finish connecting.

## How sync works

- Sync is **read-only**. Dayopt never changes anything on the Google Calendar side
- Events sync automatically every 15 minutes. Click **Sync now** for an immediate update
- Only events from the calendars you selected are brought in
- You cannot edit an event's title or time while it stays external. Turn it into a plan or record first (see below)

## Turning an event into a plan or record

Tap an external event to convert it.

- Events ending in the future become a **plan**
- Events that already ended become a **record**

You can undo this. Tap **Undo** in the confirmation that appears.

To get rid of an event you don't need, use the dismiss icon in its corner. Confirm and it stops showing up on your calendar.

## Connecting more than one account

Click **Add Google account** to connect another one — for example, a work account alongside a personal one.

## Disconnecting

Go to the Integrations tab in Settings and click **Disconnect** on the account.

- Dayopt deletes its stored tokens and connection completely
- External events you never converted disappear
- Events you already turned into plans or records stay untouched

## Troubleshooting

### "Reconnect required"

Your Google access has expired. Click **Reconnect** with the same Google account. Sync for that connection pauses until you do.

### Calendar isn't syncing

If you see a sync error, wait a moment and click **Sync now**. If that doesn't help, disconnect and reconnect the account.

### An event disappeared

If you never converted an external event and it changes or gets deleted in Google Calendar, it disappears at the next sync. Events you already converted to a plan or record are not affected.
